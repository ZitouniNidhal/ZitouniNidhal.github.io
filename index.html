Publications & Projects

Self-Training Collapse LLM Research

Studying the collapse dynamics that emerge during self-training loops in large language models. This research investigates how iterative self-improvement can degrade model quality and proposes interventions to maintain training stability across campaigns.

Code Paper Demo

Self-Designing Neural Networks Architecture Search

An automated Neural Architecture Search (NAS) framework where networks design their own topology. SDNN explores the intersection of meta-learning and evolutionary strategies to discover efficient architectures without human-guided design.

Code Demo

Gaussian Process State-Space Models Probabilistic ML

A Python framework implementing Gaussian Process State-Space Models for probabilistic time-series analysis and system identification. Combines the flexibility of GPs with state-space representations for uncertainty-aware predictions.

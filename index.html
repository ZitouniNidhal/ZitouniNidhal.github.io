About Me

I'm a PhD Candidate in AI/ML with a focus on Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Large Language Models (LLMs). My research explores the boundaries of AI systems, from self-training dynamics in LLMs to automated neural architecture search.

With a background in Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and 3D Modeling, I bridge the gap between theoretical research and real-world applications. I'm passionate about building AI systems that are efficient, interpretable, and scalable.

My work has been published in international conferences and implemented in open-source projects. I actively contribute to the AI community through GitHub and Google Scholar.

AI Researcher & Engineer specializing in Explainable AI (XAI), Machine Learning, and Agentic AI Systems.Focused on bridging the gap between algorithmic complexity and human trust, with expertise in model interpretability frameworks and autonomous multi-agent architectures. Passionate about developing transparent, reliable, and production-ready intelligent systems.

I have hands-on experience building production-ready AI systems across diverse domains — from enterprise AI platforms and candidate assessment pipelines to clinical decision support tools. I specialise in LLM orchestration, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), prompt engineering, and deploying AI services via FastAPI and Docker.

I have two accepted papers in 2026 on multi-omics data integration for precision medicine, combining deep learning with SHAP/LIME-based explanation frameworks.

I hold an M.Res in Artificial Intelligence and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Faculty of Sciences of Monastir, Tunisia.

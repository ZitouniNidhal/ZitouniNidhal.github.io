Profile

I am an AI Developer and graduate researcher with a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Monastir. My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning, large language model engineering, and scalable backend development.

I have hands-on experience building production-ready AI systems across diverse domains — from enterprise AI platforms and candidate assessment pipelines to clinical decision support tools. I specialise in LLM orchestration, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), prompt engineering, and deploying AI services via FastAPI and Docker.

My research focuses on Explainable AI (XAI) in healthcare, where I develop interpretable machine learning models for oncology and clinical genomics. I have two accepted papers in 2026 on multi-omics data integration for precision medicine, combining deep learning with SHAP/LIME-based explanation frameworks.

I hold an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Faculty of Sciences of Monastir, Tunisia.
